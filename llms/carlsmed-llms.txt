# Carlsmed

> Carlsmed (Nasdaq: CARL) is a Carlsbad, California medical technology company building personalized
> spine surgery. Its aprevo technology platform pairs AI-enabled segmentation of a patient's own
> imaging with prior-outcomes data to produce a personalized surgical plan and patient-specific
> interbody fusion devices, manufactured on a digital production line and delivered to hospitals in

## API surface

Carlsmed has no public API. As of 2026-08-09 there is no developer portal, no API reference, no
OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/MCP contract, and no `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.` or `app.` hostname on the
carlsmed.com domain. The clinical software surface — the myaprevo application through which surgeons
review, approve and track aprevo plans — ships as a credentialed iOS app distributed through the Apple
App Store; access is arranged through a Carlsmed sales representative, not through self-service signup.
The only machine-readable JSON served from carlsmed.com is the stock WordPress core REST API at
`/wp-json/` (namespaces `wp/v2`, plus Yoast, Wordfence and WP Engine plugin namespaces) — the marketing
site's CMS, not a Carlsmed product API.
